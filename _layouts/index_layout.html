On the desk this month

A narrow table, a moka pot, and a short stack of books.

Quill is written in the hour before the house wakes, at a plain oak desk facing a plaster wall, with a moka pot doing its slow work in the next room. No dual monitors, no dashboard — just a lamp, a fountain pen, and whatever I'm reading at the moment.
